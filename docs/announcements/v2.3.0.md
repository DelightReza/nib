**Install:** download `nib-2.3.0.apk` below and open it on your tablet or phone. It updates 2.x in place, and your notebooks stay.

> **Note:** 2.3.0 stores notebooks in a new way. Each notebook is converted the first time you open it, and after that older versions of Nib can't open it. Keep 2.3.0 (or newer) once you've updated.

**Big notebooks stay light**
- Nib now reads only the pages near the screen and lets go of the rest, so a notebook with hundreds of pages opens instantly and uses little memory.
- Saving writes only the pages you changed, not the whole notebook.
- Measured on a Xiaomi Pad 6 with a 300-page notebook of dense handwriting: opens in under half a second, uses about 20–35 MB while you scroll through it (was close to 200 MB), and saves a change in under 20 ms (was rewriting 100 MB every time).

**New tool bar: basic + advanced**
- The bar now holds just what you write with all day: pen, highlighter, eraser, lasso, favourite pens and colours.
- Tap the arrow at its end to slide out the advanced tools next to it: text, shapes, tape, laser pointer, images and stickers, ruler, finger writing, beautify and handwriting to text.
- The advanced row closes after you pick a tool; tap the pin to keep it open. While it's closed, the arrow shows the advanced tool you're using.
- Works with the bar on any edge of the screen.

**Shapes tool**
- Draw a line, circle, triangle or rectangle and it becomes a clean shape when you lift the pen, with handles to adjust it. Uses your pen's colour and thickness.

**Pens**
- The size dots now show the real thickness of the ink at your current zoom.
- The thickness slider covers a wider range, changes evenly from thin to thick, and shows the width in mm.

**Selecting and erasing**
- The lasso picks up strokes that are only partly inside the loop. A faint line shows how an open loop will be closed.
- Writing that runs over the edge of a page onto the next can now be erased and selected from either page.

**Split view**
- The handle between the two notebooks is now a slim grip on the dividing line: drag it to resize, tap it for options (stack or side by side, swap, equal halves, close).
- The zoom level shows over the notebook you're zooming.

**Xiaomi HyperOS + root (optional):** `nib-stylus-unblock.zip` is the same Magisk module as before (v2). It only removes HyperOS's pen-stroke delay for Nib. See [magisk-stylus-unblock](magisk-stylus-unblock/README.md).
