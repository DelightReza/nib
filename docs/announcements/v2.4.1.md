**Install:** download `nib-2.4.1.apk` below and open it on your tablet or phone. It updates 2.x in place, and your notebooks stay.

**New notebook from anywhere**
- The **+** next to your tabs and the **Split view** list now start with **New notebook**: the same screen as on the notes screen (name, notebook or infinite canvas, page style, paper, cover). It opens in a new tab, or beside the notebook you're in. It's made in the same folder as that notebook.

**Fixes**
- Tape shows up straight away. A long tape with dots could take about half a second to appear, and on lift there could be a moment with no tape at all.
- Ink can no longer get stuck off screen. In split view, a notebook could stop showing new strokes (they were saved, and appeared all at once after touching the page with a finger). Nib now checks its fast pen mode every time the pen goes down and makes sure each finished stroke reaches the screen.
- A notebook that was deleted or moved to the trash no longer opens as an empty notebook.

**For bug reports:** Nib now keeps a small log of display events in its own files, so a problem at a known time can be traced afterwards. It never leaves your device.

**Xiaomi HyperOS + root (optional):** `nib-stylus-unblock.zip` is the same Magisk module as before (v2). It only removes HyperOS's pen-stroke delay for Nib. See [magisk-stylus-unblock](magisk-stylus-unblock/README.md).
