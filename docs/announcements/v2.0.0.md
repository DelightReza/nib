**Nib 2.0 is out** ✍️ It's a free, open-source handwriting notes app for Android tablets.

New in this version:
- **Handwriting to text:** lasso what you wrote and turn it into text, in English, ਪੰਜਾਬੀ, हिन्दी or عربي
- **Convert as I write:** your writing becomes text by itself after a short pause
- **Auto language:** Nib works out which of the four you're writing
- **Change your mind:** tap converted text to read it in another language, or turn it back into handwriting
- **Beautify:** keeps your own handwriting but straightens lines and evens out size and spacing
- **New notes screen:** real page previews, small/medium/large view, and an Unfiled list
- **Select several notebooks** to move, export or delete them together
- **Trash:** deleted notebooks wait 30 days before they're gone
- **Themes:** light or dark, eight accent colours or your own, and a true-black option
