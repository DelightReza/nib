**Install:** download `nib-2.2.2.apk` below and open it on your tablet or phone. It updates 2.x in place, and your notebooks stay.

**New notebook screen**
- New now opens a sheet where you set the name, type (notebook or infinite canvas, or import a PDF), page style, paper and cover before the notebook is made. You can also make your choice the default.

**Notebook covers**
- 34 designs in 10 colours, with the title on a label. They show on the notes screen and as a page before page 1. Change one any time with Cover… in the ⋯ menu.

**More templates**
- Graph 5 mm, fine dots, hexagon, a daily planner and a weekly planner.

**Stickers**
- Import images or a .zip pack once, then place them on any page from the image button. Long-press a sticker to remove it. Stickers are included in backups.

**Zoom and reopening**
- Settings → Maximum zoom: 400, 600 or 800%.
- While you pinch, a zoom chip shows the zoom level and a lock. Locked, pinching only scrolls.
- Each notebook reopens at the page, scroll and zoom you left it at. If you leave the app from a notebook, starting it again goes straight back there, with its tabs and split.

**Phones**
- Nib now fits phone screens. Tools that don't fit fold into a "More tools" button, panels fit and scroll, the text box stays above the keyboard, and the notes screen is more compact.

**Fixes**
- The app no longer slows down or runs out of memory over time. Large notebooks open much faster, and erasing no longer stutters.
- Notebooks with a very long text box save again. Such notebooks use a new file version that older Nib versions can't open; all others still open in Nib 2.1 and later.
- Smoother writing on devices without front-buffer ink (e.g. POCO X7 Pro, MediaTek).
- The laser trail draws as a smooth line.
- Nib asks the display for its fastest refresh rate while you write.

**Xiaomi HyperOS + root (optional):** `nib-stylus-unblock.zip` is the same Magisk module as before (v2). It only removes HyperOS's pen-stroke delay for Nib. See [magisk-stylus-unblock](magisk-stylus-unblock/README.md).
