**Nib 1.2.0 is out** ✍️ It's a free, open-source handwriting notes app for Android tablets.

New in this version:
- **Hold to erase:** rest the pen for half a second and erase without switching tools
- **Erase preview:** erased ink turns grey first and goes when you lift
- **Editable shapes:** stretch a snapped shape as you draw it, and reshape it later using the handles on its corners
- **Flat highlighter tip** that straightens itself when you lift
- **Write with your finger** too, with palm rejection
- **Move the tool bar** to any edge: bottom, top, left or right
- **Tape** now looks real while you lay it

Thanks to everyone who sent feedback; most of these came from you.
