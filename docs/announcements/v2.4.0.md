**Install:** download `nib-2.4.0.apk` below and open it on your tablet or phone. It updates 2.x in place, and your notebooks stay.

> Coming from 2.2 or older? 2.3 changed how notebooks are stored: each notebook is converted the first time you open it, and older versions of Nib can't open it after that.

**Arrange your tool bar**
- Choose which tools sit in the bar and which wait in the advanced row. Long-press a tool (or tap it again) and pick **Move to the tool bar** or **Move to more tools** at the bottom of its options.
- Image, ruler, finger writing, beautify and handwriting to text: long-press them for the same choice.
- Your layout is remembered.

**Resize shapes, keeping their shape**
- A selected shape now shows a light box with square corners. Drag a corner to make the whole shape bigger or smaller: it keeps its proportions and its line thickness.
- The round handles still move a single corner or side, as before.

**Tape that looks like real tape**
- New in the tape options: **Look** — Flat (as before), Masking, Washi and Kraft.
- Masking: paper fibres and hand-torn ends. Washi: finer tears and a soft sheen, patterns show clearly. Kraft: brown paper with specks. All three cast a soft shadow.
- Tape still covers what's under it completely; tap it to peel it back, as before.
- New tape colour: Cream.

**Fixes**
- Writing that runs over the edge of a page now shows on the next page in PDF and image exports and in the page overview.
- Tapping a large or resized shape selects it reliably, and the eraser catches it anywhere along its lines.
- A notebook can no longer end up open in two tabs at once.

**Xiaomi HyperOS + root (optional):** `nib-stylus-unblock.zip` is the same Magisk module as before (v2). It only removes HyperOS's pen-stroke delay for Nib. See [magisk-stylus-unblock](magisk-stylus-unblock/README.md).
