**Install:** download `nib-2.5.0.apk` below and open it on your tablet or phone. It updates 2.x in place, and your notebooks stay.

> **On 2.4.1? Please update.** 2.4.1 could leave black patches and bits of the eraser circle on the page while erasing. 2.5.0 fixes that.

**Layers**
- Keep different kinds of writing apart: for example the lecture, your own notes, and the answers. One set of layers for the whole notebook.
- Open **Layers** from the advanced tools (or **⋯ → Layers**). Tap a layer to write on it, tap the eye to hide or show it. The **⋮** menu has rename, move up or down, merge down and delete.
- New writing, text, images, stickers and tape go on the layer you're writing on. The eraser and the lasso only touch that layer, so you can erase your notes without disturbing the lecture.
- Hide a layer to study, such as the answers, on every page at once. Hidden layers aren't shown in PDFs and images you export.
- Selected something on the wrong layer? Use **Layer** in the selection bar to move it.
- When a notebook has more than one layer, its tab shows the layer you're writing on.
- Older versions of Nib still open notebooks with layers. They show everything together.

**Fixes**
- No more black patches or eraser circle leftovers while erasing (from 2.4.1).
- The advanced tools close as soon as you pick another tool or start writing (pin them to keep them open).

**Xiaomi HyperOS + root (optional):** `nib-stylus-unblock.zip` is the same Magisk module as before (v2). It only removes HyperOS's pen-stroke delay for Nib. See [magisk-stylus-unblock](magisk-stylus-unblock/README.md).
