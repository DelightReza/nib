**Nib 2.1 is out** ✍️ It's a free, open-source handwriting notes app for Android tablets.

New in this version:
- **Your own fonts:** import any .ttf or .otf for text boxes, next to Inter, Serif, Mono and Handwriting
- **Style words, not just boxes:** select words to change their font, size, bold, italic, underline, strike-through or colour
- **Format bar while you type:** everything above the text box, showing the style at the cursor
- **Fountain pen taper:** writing with your finger, strokes now start and end thin like a real nib
